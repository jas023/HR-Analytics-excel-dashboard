# 👥 TechCorp – HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **TechCorp HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Human Resource Analytics project designed to transform employee data into meaningful workforce insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee headcount, salary distribution, performance, attendance, attrition, payroll, and employee exits across different departments, cities, and years.

The primary objective is to help HR teams understand workforce patterns, evaluate employee performance, monitor retention, and make more informed HR decisions through data-driven insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall employee strength and active workforce.
- Analyze employee distribution across departments and locations.
- Evaluate salary distribution and compensation patterns.
- Understand the relationship between employee salaries and performance scores.
- Track attendance and employee attrition rates.
- Analyze employee exits across departments and years.
- Monitor payroll expenses and employee replacement costs.
- Identify workforce trends and opportunities for HR improvement.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Headcount by Department
- Compares employee distribution across different departments.
- Identifies departments with higher and lower workforce strength.

### 2. Salary Band Distribution
- Categorizes employees into salary bands.
- Compares salary distribution across departments.
- Helps understand workforce compensation patterns.

### 3. Average Salary vs KPI Score
- Compares average employee salaries with departmental KPI scores.
- Provides insights into compensation and performance patterns.

### 4. Employee Status Distribution
- Visualizes the distribution of employees by employment status.
- Covers Active, On Leave, Resigned, and Terminated employees.

### 5. Employee Exits by Department & Year
- Tracks employee exits across departments.
- Compares workforce exits over 2022, 2023, and 2024.
- Helps identify departments experiencing higher employee turnover.

### 6. Peer Score Trend by Department & Year
- Compares departmental peer scores across three years.
- Highlights changes in performance trends over time.

### 7. Insights Corner
A dedicated insights section presents key workforce observations, including attrition patterns, employee exits, salary-performance comparisons, attendance, and female workforce representation.

## 💡 Key HR Insights

- **Employee Attrition:** The attrition rate stands at approximately 9.17%, with 55 employee exits highlighted in the dashboard.
- **Departmental Attrition:** Finance and Marketing record the highest attrition rates at approximately 12.5%.
- **Employee Retention:** Employee exits decreased from 23 in 2023 to 13 in 2024.
- **Salary vs Performance:** Engineering has relatively higher salary levels but the lowest KPI score among the compared departments.
- **Attendance:** Overall employee attendance stands at 92%.
- **Workforce Diversity:** Female employee representation is approximately 31%, with lower representation in HR, Sales, and Customer Success.
